Securing Africa against biological threats

The Biosafety & Biosecurity Initiative (BBI) strengthens the systems that prevent, detect and respond to accidental and deliberate biological threats — across all 55 African Union Member States, in line with IHR (2005), the Biological Weapons Convention and UNSCR 1540.

What we do

Six priority areas

The BBI 2021–2025 Strategic Plan organises continental action around six priority areas — two enabling efforts and four operational priorities — that turn biosafety and biosecurity into a sustained, professional discipline.

The challenge

One incident can cross borders in days

“When a single biological incident, whether accidental or deliberate, can affect multiple countries in a matter of days, the necessity for a continent-wide, harmonised biosafety and biosecurity framework becomes undeniable.”

— Dr Raji Tajudeen, Africa CDC
