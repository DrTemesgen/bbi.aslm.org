Securing Africa against biological threats

The Biosafety & Biosecurity Initiative (BBI) strengthens the systems that prevent, detect and respond to accidental and deliberate biological threats — across all 55 African Union Member States, in line with IHR (2005), the Biological Weapons Convention and UNSCR 1540.
