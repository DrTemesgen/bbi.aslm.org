The Training & Certification Programme

The Africa CDC Regional Training and Certification Program for Biosafety and Biosecurity Professionals (RTCP-BBP) — explained simply.

Overview

What the programme is

The RTCP-BBP is a continental programme of the Africa CDC Biosafety and Biosecurity Initiative (BBI), delivered with the African Society for Laboratory Medicine (ASLM). Launched in 2022, it sets a single, harmonised standard for training and certifying biosafety and biosecurity professionals across all 55 African Union Member States — turning biosafety into a recognised profession.

It uses a One Health approach (human, animal, plant and environmental sectors) and gives practitioners a portable, continent-wide credential recognised wherever they work in Africa.

Why it matters

A biological incident can cross borders in days. A common standard means every country can rely on competent, certified professionals.

🌍

Who it's for

Laboratory, health, animal-health, engineering and security professionals who work with or oversee biological materials and facilities.

    How applications are assessed

    Submissions are reviewed by the Examination & Certification Committee (ECC) against a structured evaluation matrix with weighted scoring, based on agreed standards. The committee may request clarifications and makes the final certification decision.

    Assessment & recognition

    How competency is proven and kept

    📊

    Proficiency matrices

    Each area has a proficiency matrix defining the knowledge and skills required at every level — the benchmark for assessment.

    ✅

    Competency evaluation

    Candidates are assessed with competency evaluation checklists that confirm practical, on-the-job capability.

    🛠️

    Improvement project

    Candidates demonstrate impact through an improvement project, reported in a standard format, showing real change in their setting.

    🔁

    Maintaining certification

    Credentials are kept current through continuing professional development and periodic renewal.

    Governance

    The programme is led by Africa CDC under the BBI, delivered with ASLM, and overseen by the Examination & Certification Committee (ECC) which safeguards standards and awards certification. Learn about the ECC →
